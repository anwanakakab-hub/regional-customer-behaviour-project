
# Regional Buyer Behaviour Analysis

## About the Project

This project was completed as part of the **Data Analytics Capstone Experience – Summer 2026**.

The project focused on understanding how customer buying behaviour differs across regions in Canada for a Canadian retail company. The main goal was to use sales and customer data to identify regional performance differences, customer behaviour patterns, and opportunities to increase customer value.

The analysis looked at areas such as:

* Regional sales performance
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Customer activity
* Repeat customer behaviour
* Product and order trends
* Customer segmentation
* Revenue growth opportunities
* Sales forecasting

Two different datasets were used. One contained transaction-level information such as sales, orders, products, coupons, and customer type. The second contained customer and regional information such as province, total spending, number of orders, and regional AOV.

The datasets were analyzed separately because there was no reliable common key available to safely combine them. This helped avoid creating inaccurate results.

## Project Highlights

* Identified clear differences in customer behaviour and performance across Canadian regions.

* Found a gap between the current Average Order Value and the company's target, showing an opportunity to increase the value of customer purchases.

* Identified repeat customers as an important business strength and recommended strategies to keep these customers active.

* Used customer segmentation and regional analysis to identify areas where targeted marketing and customer engagement could support growth.

* Built Power BI dashboards that turned complex sales and customer data into clear KPIs and business insights.

The analysis also showed that increasing basket value and encouraging existing customers to purchase more could be important growth opportunities. The project identified repeat customers as a strong part of the customer base.
